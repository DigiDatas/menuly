The real story behind every missed order

Answering the same question, twenty times a day

Most small businesses aren't losing sales to bad food or bad service — they're losing time and patience to the gap between a customer's question and a clear answer.

Every call, twenty questions

"What's the price?" "Is it in stock?" Staff spend the day repeating answers instead of taking the order.

Explained twice, still unclear

A server walks the table through the menu, and the guest still asks to see it again. Words alone rarely sell a dish.

Printed menus go stale fast

A price rises or an item sells out — and the printed card in front of the guest still tells yesterday's story.

Rush hour turns into chaos

Phones ring nonstop right when tables need attention most, and mistakes multiply exactly when you can least afford them.
